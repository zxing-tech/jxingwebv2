Thurai Dental — Clinic Journey

3x Increase in Lead Generation and 72% Automated Bookings.

arrow_back Back to Case Studies

The Challenge

Thurai Dental, a multi-branch dental group in Malaysia, relied on manual appointment booking via phone calls and static WhatsApp forms. This led to high staff overheads, high booking no-shows (around 18%), and lack of digital visibility in local geographic searches.

The Solution

JXING Tech deployed a localized SEO and patient portal system. This included a localized keyword campaign targeting individual dental services per clinic branch, a booking portal, and automated SMS/WhatsApp reminders.

Patient Portal

Fully responsive, interactive booking calendar integrated with clinic branch locations.

Automated Journey

WhatsApp integration utilizing Twilio APIs to prompt confirmation and cancel options.

System Built

02.Multi-Branch Real-Time Appointment Scheduling Calendar
03.WhatsApp Business Automated Reminder Integration
