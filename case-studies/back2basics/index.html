Back2Basics — Authority Platform

Rank #1 for target keywords and +420% Organic Traffic Growth.

arrow_back Back to Case Studies

The Challenge

Back2Basics, Malaysia's premier personal growth brand, was suffering from fragmented content distribution across various subdomains. They experienced extremely low search visibility, slow mobile load speeds, and highly inconsistent conversion rates on digital lead magnets. They needed a unified, high-performance portal that could build authoritative search value and capture leads systematically.

The Solution

JXING Tech designed and built a consolidated headless authority platform. We restructured the domain architecture and unified content databases into a single headless CMS.

Static Web Platform

Built using Vite and TailwindCSS to load instantly on any mobile network.

Headless CMS

Integrated Strapi CMS allowing editors to publish optimized articles seamlessly.

System Built

01.Consolidated Domain Architecture (Unified multi-subdomain SEO link equity)
02.Automated Content Schema & Rich Snippets Injection
03.Integrated Lead Capture Engine with Mailchimp automation
