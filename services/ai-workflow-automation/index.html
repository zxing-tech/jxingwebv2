Frequently asked questions

What predictive analytics can JXING build?

Suitable use cases may include demand forecasts, lead scoring, churn signals, anomaly detection, and next-best-action support. Feasibility depends on data quality, volume, history, and the decision being improved.

Does every automation need AI?

No. Deterministic rules and integrations are often safer and more reliable; AI is used only where it adds measurable value.

How does JXING reduce AI risk?

We define allowed data, decision boundaries, human approvals, evaluation criteria, monitoring, audit logs, and fallback behaviour before production use.
