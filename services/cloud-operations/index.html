Frequently asked questions

Does JXING own the client’s cloud account?

No. The client owns its cloud environment; JXING can configure and manage it under an agreed operating scope.

Is maintenance included with every build?

A suitable post-launch support and maintenance plan is proposed based on the application’s risk and operating needs.

Can JXING take over an existing application?

Yes, after a technical audit, access review, documentation handover, and stabilization plan.
