Frequently asked questions

Does JXING guarantee ROAS or lead volume?

No. Results depend on the offer, market, budget, creative, sales process, measurement quality, and competition. JXING agrees the test plan and success criteria before scaling.

Why connect performance marketing to web development?

Campaign performance depends on page speed, message continuity, conversion UX, analytics, and reliable event tracking. Building these together reduces gaps between media activity and the customer experience.

Can JXING manage only the campaigns?

A campaign-only scope may be considered after measurement, landing-page, access, and responsibility boundaries are reviewed.

Start with evidence, then build the right system.

Share the business problem, current systems, users, and desired outcome. JXING will help map the appropriate next step.
